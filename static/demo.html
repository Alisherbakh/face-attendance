How it works

About twice a second, one frame is sent to the server. OpenCV finds faces, and an LBPH model compares each face with the people it was trained on. A person is checked in after 5 matching frames in a row.

The model knows 11 people (10 from the LFW dataset and the author), so you will most likely see Unknown — that is the expected result.

Privacy: frames are processed in memory and never saved.
