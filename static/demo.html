    Seeing “Unknown”?

    That's the right answer: you aren't in the model. To see a match, upload a photo of someone it knows, such as Tony Blair, Colin Powell or George W. Bush (from the LFW dataset).

    How it works

    1. Detect. A Haar cascade finds faces in each frame, about twice a second.
    2. Normalize. Each face is cropped to 200×200 grayscale and its lighting is evened out.
    3. Compare. LBPH turns skin texture into histograms. The closest person wins if the distance is under 75.
    4. Confirm. On the admin's kiosk device, 5 matching frames in a row record a check-in.
    Privacy. Frames are processed in memory and never saved.
    Limitation. LBPH has no liveness check, so a printed photo can fool it.
